# Setting Your Vibe

Once you completed the project setup COZY 4, you can move on to getting COZY setup _for_ your project. Every game is different and COZY respects that with new atmosphere tooling.

This quick guide will show you how to begin to setup the atmosphere for COZY based on your needs. It assumes that you already have an [atmosphere-module](../using-cozy/modules/atmosphere-module/ "mention") added to your COZY instance.

## Broad Strokes

To get started, open up the Atmosphere module by clicking the icon in the module row

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (62).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you open the module, you will see a large button showing the current atmosphere preset. Clicking this will open the Atmosphere browser

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (64).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the browser, you will see a list of presets that you can choose from. Each of these have been hand-crafted to provide some great options to build your project off of. Try a few of these to see if any of them seem to suit your desired aesthetic more than others!

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Create a Preset

To create your own preset, you can take any of the parameters of the existing preset and make tweaks. As you adjust the color values, the changes will be reflected in the scene instantly. When you are done, you can either save the changes to a new profile or discard the changes.

## Fine Details

Once you have a preset you are happy with, you can exit the browser and start working with overrides directly on your atmosphere module. These let you adjust specific things directly for more granular edits.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (66).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Having trouble finding the override you are looking for? Take a look at the [features](../using-cozy/features/ "mention") list for a full list including descriptions.

## Optimization

COZY is a very well featured system and can easily have features that you will never need for your project. To improve performance, consider disabling or removing these features in the [features](../using-cozy/features/ "mention") window.
