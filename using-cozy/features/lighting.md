## Ambient

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Sets the ambient lighting color, mode and intensity.

> Ambient light, also known as diffuse environmental light, is light that is present all around the Scene and doesn’t come from any specific source object. It can be an important contributor to overall Scene illumination.
>
> [Unity Documentation](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.5/Documentation/Manual/lighting-ambient-light.html)
