# Zephyr

> Bring unified wind to every system in your game with Zephyr: Dynamic Wind System. Easily add wind to CPU (rigidbodies, audio, particle systems, C#) and GPU (shaders, VFX graphs) at once.

<p align="center"><a href="https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/particles-effects/zephyr-dynamic-wind-system-348514?aid=1011luj9X" class="button secondary">Purchase</a></p>

No installation required.

## Scene Setup

Requires an active Zephyr instance in your scene.
